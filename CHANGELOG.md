# What's new in CanalPeer

## 1.5.1 (2026-11-04)

- Android TV and Fire TV: a Paste button next to the box on the first screen. Copy a link, choose Paste and press OK to watch it, without typing it with the remote.

## 1.5.0 (2026-11-02)

- Android: works on Android 7 and 8 too. Until now it needed Android 9, and older TV boxes said "There was a problem parsing the package".
- Android TV and Fire TV: press OK on the remote to open the controls, move with the arrows and press OK again. The volume is there too. Back closes them.
- Android phones: picture in picture. Press Home, or the new button, and the channel keeps playing in a small window.
- Android: Back takes you to the app you opened the channel from. Stop still takes you to CanalPeer.
- Android: now in Spanish, French, German, Italian and Portuguese, following your device's language.
- Android: if a channel shows no picture, CanalPeer tries another way to decode it by itself. If your device still has trouble, switch "Video decoding" to Software on the first screen.
- Android: Play no longer hides behind the "Paused" message.

## 1.4.4 (2026-11-01)

- Going back or forward in a live channel no longer jumps to the wrong moment on channels with a higher quality than announced.
- Android: the update notice shows how much of the download is done.

## 1.4.3 (2026-11-01)

- Android: channels whose sound came out mute on Fire TV now play with sound.

## 1.4.2 (2026-10-31)

- Volume control in the player, on phones and on the computer.

## 1.4.1 (2026-10-31)

- Android: opens the channels of apps with their own engine, such as Ace Serve.

## 1.4.0 · desktop 1.3.0 (2026-10-31)

- Channels start in a few seconds: a slow server no longer holds up the rest.
- Keeps working, and keeps getting updates, when canalpeer.com is blocked during matches.
- From now on Android, Windows and Mac share one version number.

## 1.3.0 · desktop 1.2.0 (2026-10-29)

- Movies: they play like a channel, with a progress bar and going back and forward.
- Choose the audio track when a channel or movie has more than one.

## 1.2.0 · desktop 1.1.0 (2026-10-28)

- A new first screen to paste a channel and watch it.
- Android phones: touch controls over the video.
- Windows: a single file to download and open.

## 1.1.5 · desktop 1.0.3 (2026-10-25)

- Every start brings the newest list of known channels, so they open even when their servers are blocked.

## 1.1.4 · desktop 1.0.2 (2026-10-24)

- Hundreds of channels come built in and open even when their servers are blocked.

## 1.1.3 · desktop 1.0.1 (2026-10-24)

- Channels you have watched open again even when their servers are blocked.

## 1.1.1 · desktop 1.0.0 (2026-10-23)

- Android: CanalPeer tells you when there is a new version and installs it.
- Windows and Mac: CanalPeer for the computer, which updates itself when you close it.

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-23)

- First version for Android and Fire TV: open an acestream:// link and watch it, with no other app.
